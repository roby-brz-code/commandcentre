# 11. FP&A & Strategic Finance

## Suggested Monthly Rhythm

| When | Activity | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Month-end +3 days** | Close the books (Section 3) | Closed P&L and BS |
| **Month-end +5 days** | Prepare management pack | 1-pager: revenue, volume, margins, cash, key metrics |
| **Month-end +5 days** | Variance commentary | Month-over-month changes explained |
| **Month-end +7 days** | Share with leadership | Async Slack post or 15-min walkthrough |
| **Mid-month** | Flash estimate | Quick revenue/volume check — are we tracking to plan? |

---

## Management Pack Contents (Keep Lean)

- Revenue: total, by merchant, MoM trend
- Volume: gross processing, by merchant
- Margins: Breeze take rate, CKO cost rate, net margin
- Cash: ending positions across all accounts
- Key metrics: chargeback rate, settlement accuracy, merchant count
- Notable items: anything unusual or requiring attention

---

## Quarterly Add-Ons

- Quarterly business review (deeper analysis, trends, forward look)
- Budget vs actual (once 2026 budget is built)
- Unit economics by merchant
- Merchant diversification tracking (Stake concentration)

| Pillar | Key Initiatives |
| --- | --- |
