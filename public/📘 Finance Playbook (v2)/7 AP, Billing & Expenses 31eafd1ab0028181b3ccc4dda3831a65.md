# 7. AP, Billing & Expenses

## Accounts Payable ([Bill.com](http://Bill.com))

### Workflow

1. **Vendor invoices** → All team members forward to [Bill.com](http://Bill.com) inbox within 24 hours
2. **Review** → Finance lead verifies accuracy, coding, supporting docs
3. **Approve/Reject** → Approval workflow within [Bill.com](http://Bill.com)
4. **Schedule Payment** → Based on terms, cash flow, early payment discounts
5. **Execute Payment** → Process through [Bill.com](http://Bill.com)

### Cadence

Dedicated 45-minute AP blocks: **Tuesday** and **Thursday**. Review, approve, schedule, catch stragglers.

### Rules

- No direct vendor payments by team members
- Forward invoices within 24 hours
- Contact finance lead before forwarding if unclear

> For the full AP process including vendor setup, approval matrix, payment scheduling, and month-end AP reconciliation, see the detailed write-up in the existing Notion Section 1 (Accounts Payables + Expenses).

## Expenses & Ramp

### Process

- Review Ramp card transactions weekly (Tuesday, alongside AP)
- Process employee reimbursements
- Ensure all transactions are properly coded and receipted
- Flag any unusual or unapproved spend
